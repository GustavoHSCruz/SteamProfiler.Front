3. Public SteamProfiler website

Public data. The site reads publicly available Steam information and provides profile views, comparisons, estimates, game information and visual cards. No Steam login is required for public lookups. Private profiles, libraries or inventories are not unlocked. Looking up another public profile does not authorize harassment, impersonation or unlawful reuse of personal data. Prices, currency estimates, compatibility reports and calculated metrics may be incomplete or outdated; verify them at their source before purchasing or relying on them.

4. Hosted APIs and automation

APIs, scraping and shared capacity. Use the documented endpoints, respect rate limits, response instructions and access restrictions, and reuse cached results where appropriate. Do not evade blocks or quotas by rotating identities, addresses or sessions, exhaust the service with bulk requests, or access administrative or private endpoints without authorization. Automated collection and scraping must follow these rules and the access restrictions and published instructions for the requested route. Do not collect data to build personal dossiers, reidentify visitors, stalk people or reconstruct information made private. Contact the maintainer before proposing bulk collection beyond available limits. Integrations should handle unavailable data and temporary failures. The hosted APIs offer no uptime, permanent endpoint or support guarantee. The source-code license permits self-hosting under its own conditions; it does not grant unlimited access to our servers.

5. Duo accounts and projects

Personal studio. Duo authenticates through Steam; your Steam password stays with Steam. Protect your Steam account and sign out on shared devices. Sign-in does not provide access to private Steam data. Duo stores projects, chosen images and library observations separately from the public Profiler. Projects are private by default. Account-wide export and deletion are not yet available in the alpha; individual project deletion and sign-out are available. Contact the maintainer for account or data requests. This limitation does not remove your statutory rights. Keep exported copies of work you want to preserve: the alpha may change and storage is not an archival guarantee.

6. Duo public links and exports

Sharing is your choice. Enabling a public link lets anyone with it view the rendered composition, including the text, images and public Steam information you chose to display. The output may follow saved edits and explicit library synchronization. Revoking the link or deleting the project stops future access through that link; it cannot remove screenshots or files already saved by others. Inspect the output before sharing. Yearly reports start from actual Duo observations and do not reconstruct earlier Steam Replay history. Demo data and user-entered numbers are not verified Steam records.

7. Republishing profile images

Profile cards and images. You may share your own card or composition and images of other profiles when you have authorization or another lawful basis to do so. Public access to a profile or image does not remove privacy, likeness or copyright rights. Inspect names, avatars, identifiers and other information before republishing. Do not use cards or images to impersonate the owner, imply nonexistent endorsement, harass, engage in doxxing or disclose data for an unlawful purpose. Do not present edited values as verified Steam records. Sharing, downloading or embedding does not transfer rights to marks, avatars or game artwork. An image containing profile data may remain in external copies even after changes on Steam.

8. Removal and correction requests

How to request. If a hosted page, profile image or composition violates your rights, request review, removal or correction through feedback or the relevant repository's private security channel when sensitive information is involved. Provide the relevant URL or public identifier, describe the issue and provide a safe contact method. We may request only proportionate information needed to verify the request, never your Steam password or session token. Do not submit personal documents in public messages. We will assess the request under applicable law and, where appropriate, remove or restrict access within the service we control. Duo owners can also delete their project or revoke its public link. Making Steam data private prevents new readings of that data as provided by the source, but does not automatically erase cached responses, Duo observations or exported images. Requests to delete those records should identify the affected service. We cannot remove files from other people's devices or republications on third-party sites; contact the republisher about those copies as well. This limitation does not remove our legal obligations.

9. Companion extension

Optional browser features. Companion adds public game information and profile cards to supported Steam pages. The personal game panel is off by default and requires you to enable it; it uses a public SteamID and the current game. Do not enter someone else's SteamID while claiming to be them. You can disable features in settings or uninstall the extension. Steam page changes, browser restrictions or API outages may interrupt features. Companion does not buy games, perform trades, bypass Steam privacy settings or require Steam credentials. Its data handling is described in Companion Privacy.
