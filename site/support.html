support

This runs in my house.

The site is free, has no ads, no cookies, no trackers and sells nothing - the privacy page says exactly what that means. It runs on a server here at home, on my electricity and my Steam key. If it was useful to you and you want to throw a coin, this is where - and if you do not, use it just the same.

what I do with itno promises

None of this is a subscription and there is no supporter tier: people who give and people who do not see exactly the same site, with the same features. I am not locking anything behind a payment.

If any money shows up, it goes to the server's power bill and to a domain that comes out of my pocket today. If a lot shows up, then I think about moving this out of my house and onto something that does not go down when the neighbourhood loses power.

Rather help without spending? Send a message with a bug or an idea - that is worth the same to me.
